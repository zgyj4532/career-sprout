# UI benchmark evidence

Public official guides and demo media, not authenticated or device-tested competitor sessions.

| Reference | Observed workflow | Use in CareerSprout |
|---|---|---|
| [Europass CV guide](https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv) and user-provided frame from its CV tutorial | The frame is a dashboard: profile summary and links such as “Create a CV” are left of a broad content area. The guide separately describes selecting profile information, choosing a design, then downloading or storing the CV. | Borrow the separation between a document entry point and a focused work area. Do not infer that the frame shows the CV editor or copy the opportunities card grid. Do not impose profile completion. |
| [Teal resume walkthrough](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435724-how-to-build-your-resume-in-teal) | Import or start blank; distinguish a source resume from job-tailored versions and explicitly control synchronization. | Distinguish draft, saved version and targeted resume; do not copy syncing semantics. |
| [Google Interview Warmup demo](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/grow-with-google/interview-warmup/) | Demo shows choosing a field, answering, reviewing transcript and opening categorized insights. | Prioritize the current question; show feedback on demand without changing existing scoring rules. |
| [W3C reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) | Relocate and stack content at narrow widths without hiding functionality; deliberate comparison can benefit from two columns. | 320 CSS px reflow; reserve local paired views for actual text comparison. |
